About

Music found me at ten, glued to every request show on the radio. What began as late nights at small parties turned into a real break in 1996, when I got my shot to play at Ego's — and never looked back.

I grew up on a mix of retro and old-school English music, synth-soaked 80s hits, and my own love of hip-hop. That mixed spectrum still defines a set of mine today: beautiful synth work and brooding basslines, all riding a pronounced, foot-shuffling house beat.

“My skill is reading a room — finding the tracks that get feet moving and heads nodding, whatever the genre, tempo or decade. Don't be surprised if you're dancing before you notice.”
